Exploring public data with clear, reproducible analysis

Two worked examples from official publishers: what question I asked, how the file was aggregated, what the numbers show, and where the limits are. Built as a personal portfolio piece in November 2026 (AI-assisted workflow; methods and data are in the GitHub repo).

Northern Ireland

How much did GP prescribing cost in August 2026?

Source: Open Data NI — GP prescribing Aug 2026 · Code: src/case_studies/prescribing.py

Question

What was the scale of dispensing (items) and reimbursement (actual vs gross cost) across NI practices for one month?

Method

DuckDB over the published CSV (latin-1 headers). Items = prescription lines, not patients. Actual cost = contractor reimbursement; gross cost = list ingredient cost.

Loading findings…

Limits

One month snapshot; therapy-area cuts (e.g. BNF chapter 4) are defined in repo docs — not a clinical outcome study.

United Kingdom trade

How did crude petroleum exports move year-on-year?

Source: ONS CPA (08) tables (vintage cpapublicationq22026august.xlsx) · Code: src/case_studies/energy_trade.py

Question

On an apples-to-apples basis, what happened to CPA 06.1 Crude petroleum exports between Q2 2025 and Q2 2026 at current prices (CP)?

Method

Read quarterly CP sheets from the pinned CPA (08) workbook — product taxonomy CPA, not SITC. Compare the same row across consecutive Q2 quarters.

Loading findings…

Limits

One commodity line only; wider “energy” baskets (e.g. imports of crude+gas and refined fuels) are documented in the JSON summary for reviewers.
